About Us

India's go-to wholesale partner for soft toys and learning toys.

N8 Global Enterprises is a Delhi-based toy wholesaler and supplier serving the Indian retail trade. From our Sadar Bazar warehouse we stock thousands of plush soft toys, electronic toys and STEM educational kits — sourced from leading Indian manufacturers and select certified partners — and supply them at wholesale rates to shopkeepers, distributors, e-commerce sellers, schools and corporate gifting buyers in every corner of the country.

  • Based in Sadar Bazar, Delhi — the largest toy wholesale market in India
  • Supplying retailers, distributors, gift shops, toy stores and schools in 500+ cities and towns
  • Every product BIS-certified under the Toys (Quality Control) Order and GST-invoiced
  • Wide range of soft toys, electronic toys and STEM kits in stock — dispatched within 24–48 hours
