Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information we collect

When you submit a quote or catalog request we receive the details you choose to share: your name, company, contact information, location and requirement description, along with any files you attach to your email.

How we use it

We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry, prepare quotations, share product catalogs and fulfil orders. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

Retention and security

Inquiry records are kept for as long as needed to serve the business relationship and meet legal or accounting obligations. Access is restricted to authorised staff.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask for corrections or request deletion at any time by writing to info@n8global.in.
